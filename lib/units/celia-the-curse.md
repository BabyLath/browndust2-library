## Stats & Abilities
- Every 4s: Apply 1 [[Poison]] to the opponent.


## Why pick her

At 4 tokens she's one of the cheapest ways to add Poison debuff, especially without any stamina cost. She's also regular pick alongside Liatris and Rubia in Poison-stacking boards.
