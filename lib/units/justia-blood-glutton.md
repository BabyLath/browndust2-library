## Season notes

**Season 3:** her ★ activation condition now only counts units that perform cooldown actions. Units that act on triggers instead (enemy attacks, ally actions, battle start) no longer satisfy it. If you ran her next to trigger-based units, check her card in game before relying on the bonus.
