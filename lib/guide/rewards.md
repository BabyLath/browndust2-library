# Alea Chip shop

Board clears pay **Alea Chips**. Spend them in the Soul Wager shop, reached from the main **Shop** tab or the shortcut inside the mode.

The shop sells Refining Powder, Tear of Goddess, Gold, ★5 Recruit Contracts and Magic Crystals.

**Buy everything.** Clearing every Board gives enough chips to empty the shop, and leftover chips are wiped when the season ends. Even the contracts are worth taking if you are a veteran with nothing to spend them on yet.
