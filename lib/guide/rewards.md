# Alea Chip shop

The Soul Wager has two currencies. Don't mix them up:

| Currency | Earned from | Spent on | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Soul Tokens** | Every battle: 16 for a win, 10 for a loss | Units, tiles, gear and rerolls in the Battle Shop | One run on one Board |
| **Alea Chips** | Clearing a Board | Account resources in the Alea Chip shop | Until the season ends, then wiped |

Soul Tokens are the in-run money the rest of this guide talks about. Alea Chips are the reward for playing the mode.

!!! warning "Spend every chip before the season ends"
    Leftover Alea Chips are removed when the season ends. Clearing every Board gives enough to empty the shop, so buy everything, ★5 Recruit Contracts included. Season dates are on the [Seasons](../seasons.md) page.

<!-- swg:rewards -->

## What to buy first

If you can't clear every Board this season, the chips won't stretch to everything. Buy in this order:

1. **★5 Recruit Contracts.** The rarest thing in the shop. Even veterans can hold them for a character they don't have yet.
2. **Light Magic Crystals** and **Tear of Goddess**, whichever you're shorter on.
3. **Refining Powder.**
4. **Gold.** You'll get plenty of it elsewhere.

## Where to find the shop

Open it from the main **Shop** tab, or from the shortcut inside the Soul Wager menu.
