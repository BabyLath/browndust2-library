# Strategy

## What's been working

Stack debuffs, mainly **Poison**, and bring just enough healing to survive while the stacks do the work. Saved builds are on the [Builds](../builds/index.md) page. These picks come from the BD2 Tactical Compendium as of October 22, the day after Season 3 opened, so they may not reflect Season 3's cost changes yet.

Damage dealers that have performed well this season: **Liatris, Rodev's Star**, **Rubia, Thorn of the Desert** and **Blade, Apostle**.

Supports and debuffers with plenty of options: **Refithea, Pure White Blessing**, **Celia, The Curse**, **Levia, Overheat**, **Liberta, Onsen Manager**, **Palette, Shattered Dream**.

## Rules of thumb

**One main attacker, then support.** Stamina is shared. A single damage dealer backed by shields, buffs, heals and debuffs usually beats two damage dealers fighting over the pool.

**Spend early, reroll late.** Early phases can only roll low grades, so rerolling for a rare unit in Phase 1 burns tokens on odds that can't pay out.

**Losses aren't wasted.** A loss still pays 10 tokens and refreshes the shop with the reroll cost reset. With 5 lives, trading one early loss for a better shop can be worth it.

**Reserve before you leave.** If a piece you want is too expensive right now, reserve it so it survives the next refresh.

**Check View Formation every phase.** Enemies scale each phase. Counter what is actually there.

**Respect End Game.** If your board can't kill by around 50 seconds, you need sustain that keeps up with ramping damage.
