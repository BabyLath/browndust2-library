# Board and shop

The formation screen has three parts: **Storage** on one side, the **Main Field** in the middle, and the **Battle Shop**.

## Main Field

A 7×7 grid. Tiles go down first and range from 1×1 up to 2×2 or 1×4. Units sit on tiles and have their own footprints, from 1×1 up to an 8-square shape. A unit needs empty tile space under its whole footprint.

To rotate a piece by 90°:

- **PC:** hold left click, then press right click.
- **Mobile:** hold with one finger, tap with a second.

Some units list placement bonuses on their skill card. Those only count when the required tiles are on the field, so a bonus tile rotated off the edge does nothing.

## View Formation

Check the enemy's layout and actions before you commit. Build to counter what you see: Cleanse units against heavy debuffers, Remove units against buff stackers.

## Battle Shop

Four slots. The shop refreshes after every battle, win or lose.

**Grade odds depend on the Board and Phase.** Early phases only roll low grades. Late phases open up all six grades. Season 3 added Unique-grade pieces. See [Units](../units/index.md) for every piece and its price.

**Reserve** a piece by tapping its price tag. It stays through the next refresh. Tap again to release it.

**Discounts** show up 10% of the time at half price, rounded up. A 5-token unit on discount costs 3.

**Rerolls** cost 1 token at first and go up by 1 every 4 rolls in the same phase. Past the fourth or fifth roll you are usually better off saving. The [reroll calculator](../tools/calculators.md#rerolls) shows the running total.

**Selling** returns half the purchase price, rounded down, with a floor of 2. The Protection Tile is the one exception and sells for 1. Upgrades do not raise the sell value.

## Storage

Pieces in Storage don't fight and cost nothing to keep. Use it to hold a unit until you can afford the tiles for it. You can filter Storage by tiles, units or gear.
