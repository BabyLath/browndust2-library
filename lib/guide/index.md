# How the mode works

The Soul Wager is an auto-battler. You buy tiles, units and gear from a small shop, arrange them on a 7×7 board, then watch the fight play out on its own. The decisions all happen before the battle.

!!! tip "Your first run"
    1. **Read the season's team.** [Current season strategy](current-season.md) tells you what to buy, in what order. Follow it for your first Board.
    2. **Learn the shop.** Rerolls get more expensive, losses still pay and you can reserve pieces. Five minutes on [Board and shop](board-shop.md) saves a lot of tokens.
    3. **Know why boards lose.** Shared Stamina and End Game decide most fights. [Battle and End Game](battle.md) explains both.
    4. **Plan before you buy.** Try a layout in the [Board planner](../tools/planner.md) to check the pieces fit and what they cost.
    5. **Cash in.** Spend every Alea Chip in the [Alea Chip shop](rewards.md) before the season ends. Leftovers are wiped.

    When you want to build your own teams instead of copying one, read [How to build a team](strategy.md).

## Unlocking it

Clear every main quest in **Edge of Dimension**.

## The loop

Seasons run about eight weeks. Each season has several **Boards**, and each Board is a chain of **Phases** (single battles). Clear every Phase to clear the Board.

1. Spend Soul Tokens in the Battle Shop.
2. Place tiles on the board, then units on the tiles, then gear on the units.
3. Press Battle and let it resolve.
4. Collect tokens: **16 for a win, 10 for a loss**.

## Losing

You get **5 lives per Board**. A loss refreshes the shop and resets your reroll cost, so it doubles as a free reroll. Lose all five and the Board starts over from Phase 1.

## Clearing

Clearing a Board pays out Alea Chips for the [Alea Chip shop](rewards.md).

!!! warning "Settlement"
    Each season locks at **15:00 UTC on its last day**. Finish your runs before then. Dates are on the [Seasons](../seasons.md) page.

## Unit upgrades

Units go up to +5 inside the mode, but never past the upgrade level that character has on your main roster. A +0 Diana on your account stays +0 here.
