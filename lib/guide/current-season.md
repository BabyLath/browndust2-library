# Current season strategy

!!! tip "TL;DR"
    - **Run Curse.** One attacker, everyone else stacking [[Poison]] & [[Frost]], a healer to stay alive.
    - **Your core Costumes:** Celia (The Curse) & Nebris (Labyrinth Gatekeeper)
    - **Get cleanse and remove.** This season's enemy formations steals your buffs and stacks [[Laceration]] on you, so don't forget to bring  [[Cleanse]] & [[Remove]].
    - **Avoid Stacking Buffs.** Since the enemy steals your buffs, stacking buffs would just be giving away free buffs to the enemy.


    - **Before the season ends, spend every Alea Chip.** Leftovers are wiped.

    - **Note:** This is just one of the many other possible strategies for this season.

No theory, just what to do for this season. If you want to know *why*, read [How to build a team](strategy.md).

## What to buy first

Celia, Refithea, Liatris and Rubia are Basic or Rare grade, so they can show up from Phase 1. Buy in this order:

1. **Celia, The Curse**. Your cheap poison unit. Buy on sight.
2. **Liatris or Rubia** (6). Whichever comes first. Don't buy both.
3. **Knight's Necklace** (5). Goes on Celia, nobody else.
4. **Mercenary Staff or Sword** (4). First one on your attacker, second on Refithea.
5. **Protection Tiles** (2). Whenever you need floor.
6. **Refithea, Pure White Blessing** (4) and **Liberta, Onsen Manager** (6).
7. **Curse Tile** (6). Under Celia.
8. **Upgrades**, once the board above is complete.

**Can't afford it?** Tap the price tag to reserve it. It stays through the next shop refresh.

## Phase by phase

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| **Early Phases** | Shops only roll low grades. Buy Celia and other core units, also great to expand your tiles early. |
| **Middle Phases** | Make sure you atleast have some core units working and sustain. Don't forget your cleanse and remove. Check View Formation before every battle. |
| **Late Phases** | All grades show up. Fill gaps, upgrade your Core Units, and reroll up to 4 times if you're still missing a core piece. |
| **Lost a battle?** | Not a disaster. You still get 10 tokens, the shop refreshes and rerolls reset to 1. You have 5 lives per Board. |

## If the enemy has…

Open **View Formation** before every battle. If you see one of these, change one piece:

| Enemy board | Fix |
|---|---|
| Lots of debuffs on you | Add a [[Cleanse]] unit. Refithea (Poolside Fairy) costs 4. |
| Lots of buffs or Energy Guard | Add a [[Remove]] unit. Scheherazade (Pool Party) costs 5 and heals. |
| [[Cleanse]] units | Your Poison gets wiped. Add [[Frost]] & [[Laceration]] so they act less. |
| Fights that drag past a minute | Add more Protection Tiles. End Game is hitting 16 a second by then. |

Keep your counter unit in **Storage** between battles. Storage is free.

## Don't

- Don't run multiple attackers without enough stamina.
- Don't put the wrong gear on wrong units.
- Don't sell to change plans. You only get half back.
