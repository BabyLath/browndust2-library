# Board planner

Try a board before you spend anything. Drag a tile from the list onto the board, then drag units onto the tiles and add gear to them. Clicking works too: click a piece in the list, then click the board. The planner checks that every piece fits and adds up the cost.

<!-- swg:planner -->

## How it works

- **Tiles first.** A unit needs a tile under every square of its footprint, the same as in game.
- **Drag to move.** Drag any piece on the board to a new spot. Drop it off the board and it goes back.
- **Drag to remove.** Drop a piece from the board onto the list on the right and it's removed. A tile takes everything on it with it. The message after removing has an Undo. Dragging a tile with units on it moves them together. If a unit also stands on other tiles, those tiles and anything on them come along too. A tile with units on it can't be rotated. On a phone, drag pieces out of the list by their shape icon; swiping anywhere else on the list scrolls it.
- **Rotate** with the R key (also mid-drag) or the Rotate button, while placing or after you've clicked a placed piece. Esc stops placing.
- **Gear** shows which of its two effects the unit gets. Weapons have a good effect either way. Armor and Knight's Necklace are much weaker on the wrong tag, and the planner marks that in red. A unit with both an attack tag and Support, like Liatris, is treated as both; check that in game before relying on it.
- **Your plan is saved** in this browser and in the page address. Copy link to share it; anyone who opens the link sees the same board.
- **Copy board block** gives you a block to paste into a build page. See "Writing a build" in the README.

Prices are this season's, from the [Units](../units/index.md), [Tiles](../reference/tiles.md) and [Gear](../reference/gear.md) pages. The total is before discounts and upgrades.
