# Board planner

Try a board before you spend anything. Drag a tile from the list onto the board, then drag units onto the tiles and add gear to them. Clicking works too: click a piece in the list, then click the board. The planner checks that every piece fits and adds up the cost.

<!-- swg:planner -->

## How it works

- **Tiles first.** A unit needs a tile under every square of its footprint, the same as in game.
- **Drag to move.** Drag any piece on the board to a new spot. Drop it off the board and it goes back.
- **Drag to remove.** Drop a piece from the board onto the list on the right and it's removed. A tile takes everything on it with it. Dragging a tile with units on it moves them together. If a unit also stands on other tiles, those tiles and anything on them come along too. A tile with units on it can't be rotated. On a phone the list is a drawer along the bottom of the screen: tap its bar to open or close it, drag pieces out by their shape icon (swiping anywhere else scrolls the list), and drop a piece on the bar to remove it.
- **Undo and Redo** are next to the Name box, or press Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z (Cmd on a Mac; Ctrl+Y also redoes). They step back through every change to the pieces, including Clear board. Opening someone's link replaces your board; Undo brings yours back.
- **Rotate** with the R key (also mid-drag) or the Rotate button, while placing or after you've clicked a placed piece. Esc stops placing.
- **Gear** shows which of its two effects the unit gets. Weapons have a good effect either way. Armor and Knight's Necklace are much weaker on the wrong tag, and the planner marks that in red. A unit with both an attack tag and Support, like Liatris, is treated as both; check that in game before relying on it.
- **Open in planner** on a team card anywhere on the site loads that team here. Pieces the card doesn't give a position for wait in a **To place** tray above the board: drag or click each one onto the board, tiles first. The × beside a piece discards it.
- **Add to planner** on a unit's guide or in the Units table puts that unit in the tray without leaving the page, so you can collect several and place them here.
- **Your plan is saved** in this browser and in the page address. Copy link to share it; anyone who opens the link sees the same board.
- **Saved boards** keeps several boards in this browser. Name the board and press Save board; saving again under the same name updates it. Click a saved board's name to open it (Undo takes you back to the board you had).
- **Save image** downloads a picture of the board with its portraits, the total and every piece's gear, ready to post on Discord.
- **Copy board block** gives you a block to paste into a build page. See "Writing a build" in the README.

Prices are this season's, from the [Units](../units/index.md), [Tiles](../reference/tiles.md) and [Gear](../reference/gear.md) pages. The total is before discounts and upgrades.
