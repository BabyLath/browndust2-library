## New here

The Soul Wager unlocks after you clear every Edge of Dimension main quest. Read [how the mode works](guide/index.md) first, then [board and shop](guide/board-shop.md). Those two pages cover most of what decides a run.

## Looking something up

- [Units](units/index.md): every unit piece with price, sale price and sell value, filterable by grade and tag.
- [Gear](reference/gear.md), [tiles](reference/tiles.md) and [buffs and debuffs](reference/statuses.md).
- [Calculators](tools/calculators.md): reroll cost, sell value and End Game damage at any second.
